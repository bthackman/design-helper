Where it lands

A · Inglewood leads by a point: the closest to the city, with room on the lawn beside the lot, but a medium flood risk and rail next door. B · Glenmore Landing and C · Loch McKinnon tie just behind: B keeps the city close; C has the most room and is private land rather than park. D · Glenbow Ranch trails on services and access.
