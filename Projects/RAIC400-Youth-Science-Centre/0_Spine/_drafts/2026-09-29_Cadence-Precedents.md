# Cadence Precedents: Cairn Youth Centre

Draft, 2026-09-29. The question: what session cadence and group setup do real comparable programs use? We are testing the proposed cadence: 32 students, 4-week sessions in July and August, 2-week sessions the rest of the year, and groups of 8 going out to camp one at a time.

**Method.** Every fact below was seen on a fetched page from the program's own site. Anything marked *(derived)* is my own arithmetic from facts on that page. "n/s" means the fetched page didn't say. Pages that wouldn't load are listed at the end.

## Synthesis

The evidence shows three distinct cadences:

- **School classes.** Visiting classes stay 3–5 days, year-round, on fixed weekday slots: Monday to Friday, or split into Monday–Wednesday and Wednesday–Friday (Craigower, Strathcona, NatureBridge). Field groups run 8–15 per instructor (HMI 8–10, Shad 1:8, Strathcona 1:10).
- **Individual summer enrolment.** Summer programs run 2–4 weeks. They use either fixed 4-week terms (HMI Summer, Shad), stackable 2-week blocks (Les Elfes), or rolling weekly Sunday starts (ILSC, CISS).
- **Semesters.** The ~1/3 outdoor share appears only in semester schools: HMI spends about 5 of ~17 weeks on expedition.

**Is the proposed cadence supported?** The 4-week summer term is. School-year 2-week sessions are not: no fetched source ran one.

**Better pattern.** Run 1-week (Mon–Fri) school-class residencies from September to June, and 2-week summer blocks that students can stack to 4 weeks.

**Occupancy trade-off.** Weekly turnover fills more beds but leaves weekends empty. With 1-week stays, four groups of 8 can't each go out to camp. Fixed 4-week terms protect the camp rotation, but students who want shorter stays won't come.
