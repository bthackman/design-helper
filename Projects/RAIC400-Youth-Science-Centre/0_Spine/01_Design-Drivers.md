# Design drivers — Cairn Youth Centre (RAIC 400)

**Status: not yet locked.** Per this project's process-order adaptation (instructor requires
client → precedents → site, not the tool's default site-before-drivers order — see
`02_Decision-Log.md`), the drivers below are a **working/provisional set drafted to frame
Precedent Study**, not a lock. They still need the site-argues-back workshop before they freeze —
change the status line to `**Status: LOCKED YYYY-MM-DD**` only after that runs.

3–7 big ideas that steer every phase. Each phase output gets tested against these. Keep them few, specific, and testable — "daylight to every workspace" not "good design."

| W1 | **Built to rotate** *(restated 2026-09-29 for the reframe)*. One simple rotation: 32 beds in 4 nodes of 8, and one node at a time is out in the field while three are in the building. Sessions turn over every 2 weeks (school year) or 4 weeks (summer). A node's rooms stay held while it's out. | Structural (derived from the annual operating model) | Each node is independently operable: its own threshold, its two pods and washroom, a leader suite, no through-traffic. The node that's out can go quiet without darkening the other three. Stone Day (a stone added to the entry cairn at the end of each session) and the field-record wall make the rotation visible. | W2 | **Method follows setting.** Inside, the subject is tech-forward (robotics, engineering, lab science); outside, it's field science (environmental studies, biology). Academics and making are two-thirds of the program, the outdoors one-third. | Stated (Ben's direct instruction on science sub-focus; reconfirmed 2026-09-29) | Fails if the outdoor basecamp/teaching yard is just "the indoor curriculum, outside". Passes if what a node builds in the maker space or measures in the lab is used on its trip or feeds the field record, and the outdoor program teaches what the labs can't. | `02_Decision-Log.md` science sub-focus decision; 2026-09-29 reframe |
| W3 | **Two doors: arrival and trail** *(rewritten 2026-09-29; was "One front door, two postures")*. The arrival door faces the road and the community: coaches, luggage, visiting teachers, the public gym. The trail door faces the land: a node leaves for its trip and comes back muddy through the expedition zone (packing room, drying room, gear wash). The expedition zone is the hinge between them. | Contradiction (clean arrival vs. muddy return; see `04_Client-Profile.md` contradictions) | Fails if coach arrivals and luggage cross wet-gear flow, or a returning node has to walk through the public side to reach showers. Passes if the public side (gym, entry hub) can open to neighbours without entering the nodes. Works on a city site too: the trail door then opens to the site's own ecology. | `00_Brief.md` v0.7 site requirements; `04_Client-Profile.md` row 5 |

Passes if staff would defend the basecamp and gym as a real alternative, and a node that can't go out keeps its own rooms, so the rotation doesn't collapse. | `00_Brief.md` v0.7 fallback paragraph; `04_Client-Profile.md` row 11 |

## Consolidation workshop (run once, at the drivers-lock gate — see `phase-0-drivers.md` §4)
Every candidate driver tested against the verified site facts, one row each, before anything locks.
Full method in `phase-0-drivers.md`; this is just the table it currently has no template home.

| Candidate driver | Evidence tier (stated/derived/structural/contradiction) | Site fact tested against | Verdict | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
|  |  |  | CONFIRM / SPECIFY / DEFLATE-REFRAME / DEMOTE |  |

**Verdict** — CONFIRM (site strengthens it, lock as-is) · SPECIFY (site adds precision, lock
sharpened) · DEFLATE-REFRAME (site makes part of it moot, salvage what survives) · DEMOTE (right
idea, wrong scale — send to the relevant phase as a quality target, not a project driver).
**Disposition** — where it actually went: locked as-is / locked-reframed-into [driver #] /
demoted-to [phase] / dropped-because [reason]. Nothing on this table may be left blank — the "full
disposition" lock-quality gate means every candidate's fate is traceable, not just the winners'.
