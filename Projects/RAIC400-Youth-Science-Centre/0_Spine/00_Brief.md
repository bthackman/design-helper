# Brief — Cairn Youth Centre: Academics & Outdoors (RAIC 400)

*Version: 0.7 · Date: 2026-09-29 · Source: RAIC 400 course guidelines (rubric/reference only) + instructor feedback (class, 2026-09-28) + Ben's direction, interrogated. Client: Cairn Youth Centre (hypothetical), see `04_Client-Profile.md`.*

## Project summary
A "Collective Habitat" studio project for RAIC 400 (Foundations of Architectural Design). **Reframed 2026-09-29 after instructor feedback:** Cairn is now a **year-round residential youth centre for visiting students**, domestic and international, who come to the Calgary region for academics, making and the mountains. The low-income and social-services mission is dropped: no sanctuary suite, counselling, after-school care or Children's Services. It is still an academic building with maker spaces, not a public science centre or an expedition school. **About two-thirds of the program is academics and making in the building; about one-third is outdoors.**

**Mission:** "Cairn builds capable young people, in the lab, the workshop and the wild."

The building runs on **one simple rotation**: 32 beds in 4 nodes of 8, and one node at a time is out camping while the other three are in the building. Sessions run 2 weeks through the school year and 4 weeks in summer. Settled: 32 beds, 4 nodes, ages 12–15, gross ≈ 2,475 m². Still open: the site, city or rural (see `03_Open-Questions.md` OQ 3). The course's community-interface requirement applies either way.

## Annual operating model (v0.7, 2026-09-29)
| Period | Session | Who | Ages | Beds | Out in the field |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sept–mid-Dec** (~14 wks) | **2-week sessions**, 7 per term | A visiting class or exchange group of up to 32, plus their own teachers (2–4) | 12–15 | 32 | One node of 8 at a time, about 3 days / 2 nights each, rotating through the session |
| **Christmas** (~2 wks) | Closed | — | — | — | — |
| **Jan–mid-June** (~22 wks) | **2-week sessions**, 11 per term | Same | 12–15 | 32 | Same. Winter trips are snowshoe, ski and hut-based (OQ 18) |
| **Late June** (~2 wks) | Staff training and reset | Staff | — | — | — |
| **Jul–Aug** (8 wks) | **Two 4-week sessions** | 32 individually enrolled campers, domestic and international, in 4 nodes of 8 | 12–15 | 32 | Week 1 all in. Weeks 2–4: one node at a time on a 5-day expedition |
| **Late Aug** (~1 wk) | Reset | — | — | — | — |

**How a 2-week session runs.** Arrive Sunday, depart the second Saturday: 12 program days. Days 1–2 everyone is in (orientation, city day, first builds). Days 3–11, the four nodes take turns on a 2-night trip (about 2½ days out), back to back, so one node is always out and three are always in. The three in-building nodes (24 kids) rotate in groups of 8 through the lab, the maker space and a classroom. The last day is a showcase and Stone Day with everyone back. **Outdoor share:** field trip days plus on-site field science and day hikes come to about a third of each kid's time.

**Occupancy logic.** A node's beds stay held while it's out, so there is no bed-sharing or luggage shuffle, and the building is never more than a quarter "empty". About 18 school-year sessions × 32 ≈ 576 students, plus 64 summer campers, a year. Calgary itself is part of the program: a city day or two per session (museums, the zoo, the university), which a city site makes easy and a rural site makes a coach trip.

**Weather and wildfire-smoke fallback.** If a trip can't run, that node stays on site and uses the outdoor basecamp (fire circle, tent platforms, teaching yard) or the gym, and keeps its own rooms. The rotation doesn't collapse because the beds were never reassigned.

**Funding, in one line:** program fees from schools, exchange programs and summer families run it; a founding gift from Rocky Pyle built it.

## Site requirements (v0.7)
Exterior scope, not in the building totals.
- **Arrival:** coach lay-by and turnaround (school classes and airport transfers arrive by coach, with luggage); a small drop-off for summer families; van fleet parking for 5–10 next to the garage; staff parking for ~15; a small visitor lot.
- **Service:** kitchen loading at shipping and receiving; a concealed waste enclosure; the garage door onto a service drive. The service route never crosses arrival or play.
- **Outdoors:** outdoor basecamp (fire circle, tent platforms, teaching yard); field-science ground with real ecology on or next to the site; a trail or open land at the door.
- **Two doors:** an arrival door facing the road and the community, and a trail door where a node leaves and comes back through the expedition zone (driver W3).
- **Rural sites only:** well and wastewater field, FireSmart defensible space, two routes out.
- **Transit is no longer a requirement.** Coach access to the airport, the city's museums, zoo and university, and the mountains is the real test (see `2_Site/Site-Search.md`, 2026-09-29 reframe).

## Constraints
- Course constraints: instructor-reviewed (not code-enforced as a real permit set), medium-scale per the course's own framing, must demonstrate hierarchical spaces (private→semi-private→public), three distinct circulation classes (public/open, semi-public/restricted, private/closed), long-span/short-span program rationalization, and general (not detailed) building-systems integration.
- Deliverable cadence: Design Brief → Schematic Design (interim review) → Final presentation, plus a mandatory sketchbook throughout.
- Grading: three weighted components per assignment (Ideas / Connections / Extensions).
- **Instructor direction (2026-09-28):** simplify the client to a year-round camp program; drop the low-income focus; rural sites (Bragg Creek, Cochrane, or en route) are open. This knowingly departs from the YOUCAN sample's "centrally located" and "close to public transit" (Guidelines p. 11). The course's own examples include hostels and small student residences (p. 9), and the YOUCAN sample already calls for exchange programs "in Canada and abroad" (p. 10).
- Site: not yet chosen. City sites stay in the running alongside rural ones (Ben, 2026-09-29). Budget and schedule: not part of this exercise.

## Assumptions & gaps
- Client: **Cairn Youth Centre** (hypothetical), see `04_Client-Profile.md`. Name kept for now (Ben, 2026-09-29).
