## Phase gate — skeptic pass
If value engineering cuts 10%, which material goes first, and does the palette survive it? Which
material is doing driver work, and which is just taste? What will this look like after ten
winters? Read the locked choice against the massing and site strategy — does the material logic
reinforce the parti (e.g. heavy base where the building meets the ground), or fight it? A loop
back to phase 3 here, on paper, is normal and cheaper than the same discovery in DD.
