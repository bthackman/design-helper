### FIND layer — campaigns
A campaign = **interrogation questions** + discriminator vocabulary × region rings × source dialects, with a stop rule.

**Every campaign carries 1–3 interrogation questions** — the design questions its finds must answer ("how does the pool room meet the house?", "what's the section at the forest edge?"). A precedent that can't answer its campaign's questions is a scrapbook entry, not a card.

**Vocabulary rarity is relative to the corpus:** rare terms for open-web search; common terms are fine (often better) inside domain-restricted searches. Include other languages relevant to the rings.

### Three-pass execution (efficiency structure)
- **Pass 0 — knowledge dump:** brainstorm candidates from the assistant's internal knowledge. Free, instant, canon-deep. Every item tagged `unverified`. This is a legitimate source *only because it's labeled and then verified* — never presented raw.
- **Pass 1 — verification:** web calls confirm Pass-0 items (existence, facts, live links, images). Cheap per item.
- **Pass 2 — discovery:** web sweeps aimed where internal knowledge is thin — rings 0–1, small firms, builder/developer work, anything recent. (The assistant's blind spots and the flagship-press bias are the same blind spot; discovery concentrates exactly where local-first points.)
- Prefer **domain-restricted queries** (search inside the dialect table's domains) over open-web queries. Campaigns may run as **parallel research agents**, one per campaign.

### Region rings (start local, escalate out; exhaust inner before outer)
0 project city → 1 province/region → 2 adjacent regions → 3 country → 4 same climate anywhere → 5 anywhere (feature decoupled from climate, or lineage campaigns).

### SCORE layer — the cull scorecard (visible, whole long-list)
Per-item columns: every driver · user vectors (lineage fit, material transferability) · anti-precedent check · climate/typology fit · **documentation quality (plans? sections? details? photos-only ⇒ Evidence-tier cap)** · **visitable? (Ring 0–1 bonus — flag anything the user can stand in or drive past)** · role.

**Role taxonomy:** **Anchor** (whole building, ≥3 drivers) · **Lesson** (1–2 drivers, one move to steal) · **Evidence** (existence proof / technical reference; never presented as design precedent).

**Set-level audit (before presenting the cull):** era spread? budget spread? climate spread? any driver with zero support? — a diverse set survives review; a monoculture doesn't.

### Outputs (three kinds, not one)
1. **Cards** → Precedent-Board.md (answering their campaign's interrogation questions)
2. **Lessons** → synthesized transferable moves
3. **Human leads** → people/organizations who hold unpublished knowledge (the local firm that did the type, the builder who renovated the landmark, the association archivist) — the user can call; the tool cannot. Log with what to ask them.

### Verification rules (non-negotiable)
Breadth before judgment · fetch before cite (links opened, images confirmed viewable) · audit trail (every query logged: campaign, pass, ring, dialect, yield — nulls included) · **global time-box** (set a query/effort budget at sign-off; when spent, design with what you have — the search serves the design, not the reverse).
