# Phase 5 — Materiality

Output: `5_Materiality/Material-Palette.md` — a palette organized by system (envelope, structure, interior), each material justified by driver or precedent, with performance/cost/carbon flags.

## Start from the thinking, not the swatch
Pull forward: material lessons from the precedent board, climate realities from the site facts, budget constraints from the brief, and the drivers. Check `Library/Materials/` for materials already vetted on past projects. Propose a palette as a coherent argument ("robust civic base, light warm upper body") rather than a shopping list.

## For each material
Record application, the *why* (link to driver or precedent card), cost tier (relative is fine: $, $$, $$$), and performance notes — durability in this climate, maintenance burden, embodied carbon where it matters. Flag mismatches honestly: the beloved precedent material that won't survive freeze-thaw, the budget that can't carry the curtain wall. Unresolved flags go to open questions.

## Coherence check
Read the palette against the massing and site strategy: does the material logic reinforce the parti (e.g. heavy base where the building meets the ground) or fight it? Materials are the last phase but they argue with every earlier one — loops back to phase 3 are normal and worth doing on paper here rather than in DD later.

## Gate questions worth asking
If value engineering cuts 10%, which material goes first and does the palette survive it? Which material is doing driver work, and which is just taste? What will this look like after ten winters?
